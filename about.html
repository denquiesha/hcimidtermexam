About the College of Communication and Information Technology

Meet our leadership team and dedicated faculty driving innovation and excellence in computing education.

The College of Communication and Information Technology (CCIT) of PRMSU is a vibrant and innovative community of future IT professionals and digital communicators, known for its active student leadership, strong academic culture, and dynamic participation in competitions and campus-wide events. Home to organizations like the CCIT-SBO, DIGITS, ASCII, and The Cipher publication, the college fosters creativity, collaboration, and technical excellence through assemblies, tech-focused webinars, student-led projects, journalism initiatives, and competitive activities—from software development efforts to regional e-sports tournaments. CCIT continues to shape skilled, confident, and industry-ready graduates who lead, communicate, and innovate in the digital world.

College Goals

  1. To produce graduates who are globally competitive and socially responsible professionals in communication and information technology;
  2. To develop innovative leaders capable of addressing industry challenges through cutting-edge research and solutions;
  3. To foster a culture of excellence, ethics, and lifelong learning in the digital age.
IT

Bachelor of Science in Information Technology

The program aims to:

  1. Prepare graduates to be equipped with the principles and theories of computing leading to design and develop technological solutions for the industry, government and academe;
  2. Develop globally competitive and well-rounded IT professionals for business solutions through innovations in the global digital community; and
  3. Produce ethically responsible leaders in the management of computing technologies.
CS

Bachelor of Science in Computer Science

The program aims to:

  1. Prepare graduates to be equipped with the principles and theories of computing leading to the conduct of research and advanced studies;
  2. Develop globally competitive and well-rounded CS professionals and researchers, proficient in designing and developing computing solutions; and
  3. Produce morally upright leaders in the fast-changing ICT industry.
